# Typography

## Preferred family

**Inter** is the preferred Hazuri typeface.

Recommended weights:

- 400 — body text and supporting copy
- 500 — UI labels
- 600 — headings
- 700/800 — wordmarks and strong display headings

**Poppins** may be used as an alternative display family.

Font files are deliberately not included in this brand kit. Use the upstream/open-source distribution of the selected typeface.

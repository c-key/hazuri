# Hazuri brand guide

## Name

**Hazuri** is a coined name inspired by *hazina* (Swahili: treasure) and **URI** (Uniform Resource Identifier).

Pronunciation: **Ha-zu-ri**.

## Primary copy

**Hazuri — your personal collection of URIs.**

Architecture phrase: **Hazuri is a small, portable home for your URIs.**

## Wordmark

The official spelling is **Hazuri**. The wordmark may visually emphasize **URI** as **HazURI** through color only.

Use the standard wordmark for README files, websites and general communication. The connection-line wordmark is a special-use variant.

## Symbol

The standalone symbol is an abstract H: two vertical rounded bars connected by a line and node. It represents one home with many connections.

## Colors

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Primary / Midnight | `#0F172A` |
| Accent / Indigo | `#6366F1` |
| Accent Light | `#A5B4FC` |
| Neutral | `#E5E7EB` |

## Typography

Inter is the preferred typeface. Poppins is an acceptable alternative for display use. Font files are intentionally not bundled with this repository.

## Usage

Keep clear space around the wordmark and symbol. Do not distort, rotate, recolor outside the approved palette, or change the relative geometry of the symbol.
